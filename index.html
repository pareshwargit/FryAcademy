Fry Academy

An academy for ad filmmakers who are brilliant at the craft but were never taught the business.

We teach what film school skips:

  1. 01How to price and cost a project
  2. 02Pitch and win clients
  3. 03Protect your money with contracts
  4. 04Turn one-off jobs into retainers
  5. 05Use AI to work faster
